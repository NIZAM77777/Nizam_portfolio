About Me

Unity Game Developer & Gameplay Programmer

I'm Mohammed Nizam K N, a passionate Unity Game Developer, Gameplay Programmer, and the founder of MN7 Studios. I specialize in developing engaging 2D and 3D mobile games using Unity and C#, with a strong focus on gameplay programming, game architecture, AI systems, UI development, and performance optimization.

Over the past few Months, I have independently designed and developed multiple Unity projects, including puzzle, board, and arcade games such as Mirror Escape, Tic Tac Toe, and Four Fusion. From concept and level design to programming, testing, and publishing, I enjoy managing the complete game development process while continuously improving my skills and exploring modern game development techniques.

My goal is to create polished, enjoyable, and optimized gaming experiences for players while contributing to professional game development teams. I am always eager to learn new technologies, solve challenging gameplay problems, and build innovative games that deliver memorable player experiences.
